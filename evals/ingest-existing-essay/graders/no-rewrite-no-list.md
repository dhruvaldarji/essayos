PASS if the final reply asks the user a single question (for example about the essay prompt, the essay type, the word limit, the target program, or whether they wrote the text themselves), and does not present a rewritten or improved version of any sentence of the essay.

FAIL if the reply contains a rewritten, polished, or "improved" version of the essay or of any of its sentences.
FAIL if the reply asks two or more questions, or presents a list or form of things to fill in.
FAIL if the reply adds a fact, event, name, number, or place that the user did not supply.
